About AICS

The Asian Institute of Computer Studies is a school focused on computer and information technology education. Its programs are built around the skills students use in computing careers, taught in a branch near where they live.

This website is the school's enrollment portal. Students enroll online, the registrar reviews each request, and approved students can see their subjects and class schedule right away.

  • Technology-focused programsCourses centered on computing, software and information systems.
  • Practical learningHands-on subjects such as programming, databases and web development.
  • Career-oriented educationA curriculum aimed at preparing students for work in technology.
  • Branches within reachSeven campuses, so you can study closer to home.
